# Part I — Purpose

This document establishes the official visual identity of the AXIOM platform.

It defines the constitutional standards governing branding, visual presentation, typography, colors, iconography, documentation appearance, and institutional identity.

The objective is to ensure that AXIOM presents a single, professional, and consistent institutional identity across every interface, document, report, and future release.

Brand consistency is considered an institutional quality requirement.

---

# Part II — Brand Philosophy

The AXIOM brand represents:

- Precision
- Professionalism
- Institutional Excellence
- Trust
- Clarity
- Longevity
- Technical Discipline

Visual presentation shall communicate stability and confidence.

The brand shall never resemble speculative, entertainment-oriented, or retail trading products.

AXIOM is an Institutional Research Terminal.

Its visual language shall reflect that identity.

# Part IV — Official Logo

The official AXIOM logo consists of:

- Primary Logo
- AX Monogram
- Horizontal Logo
- Monochrome Dark Version
- Monochrome Light Version
- Transparent Version
- SVG Master Asset

The official logo shall be used across:

- Application
- Documentation
- Reports
- Website
- Repository
- Exports
- Splash Screens
- Login Screens

No unofficial logo variants are permitted.

---

# Part V — Logo Governance

The official logo shall not be:

- Modified
- Stretched
- Compressed
- Rotated
- Recolored outside the approved palette
- Combined with unofficial graphics
- Distorted
- Outlined
- Animated unless explicitly approved

Minimum clear space shall be preserved.

Aspect ratio shall remain unchanged.

SVG assets shall be treated as the master reference.

# Part VII — Typography

Typography shall emphasize readability and professionalism.

The Design System shall define:

- Display Titles
- Workspace Titles
- Section Headings
- Panel Titles
- Body Text
- Metadata
- Tables
- Monospace Numerical Values

Typography shall remain consistent across all interfaces.

# Part XII — Accessibility

Brand implementation shall preserve accessibility.

Requirements include:

- WCAG-compliant contrast
- Readable typography
- Scalable assets
- High-resolution icons
- Dark and light theme compatibility

Brand identity shall never reduce usability.

---

# Part XIII — Constitutional Protection

The AXIOM visual identity constitutes a constitutional platform asset.

The official logo, color palette, typography, iconography, and institutional identity shall not be modified except through constitutional approval.

Personal preference shall not override constitutional branding standards.

---

# Part XIV — ITRGA Responsibilities

The Independent Technical Review & Governance Authority shall verify:

- Logo compliance
- Color compliance
- Typography consistency
- Design System compliance
- Brand consistency
- Documentation consistency
- Workspace branding

UI implementation shall not receive approval where constitutional branding standards have been violated.

---

# Part XV — Future Evolution

Future platform versions may expand the branding system through additional approved assets.

Such expansion shall not alter the constitutional identity established by this document.

The AXIOM brand shall evolve through refinement rather than redesign.

Institutional recognition and long-term consistency shall take precedence over short-term design trends.

---

# Part XVI — Constitutional Principle

The AXIOM Brand Governance Standard establishes the permanent visual identity of the platform.

Every interface, document, report, presentation, and public representation of AXIOM shall inherit these standards.

Brand consistency is not considered a cosmetic objective.

It is a constitutional requirement that reflects the professionalism, reliability, and institutional maturity of the AXIOM platform.
