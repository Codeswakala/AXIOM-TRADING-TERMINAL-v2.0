# Purpose

This document establishes the constitutional hierarchy governing every engineering decision, investigation, implementation, and review performed within the AXIOM program.

No engineering activity shall contradict a higher constitutional authority.

When conflicts occur between documents, the document with the higher constitutional authority shall always prevail.

This hierarchy shall govern both the Development Authority and the Independent Technical Review & Governance Authority (ITRGA).

# Constitutional Conflict Resolution

Where two documents appear to conflict:

1. The document with the higher constitutional authority shall prevail.

2. The lower-level document shall be amended.

3. No implementation shall proceed until the conflict is resolved.

4. The ITRGA shall document every constitutional conflict identified during investigation.

5. Governance amendments shall preserve consistency throughout the constitutional hierarchy.

# Constitutional Principle

AXIOM shall be governed from constitutional principles downward rather than from implementation upward.

Engineering shall serve governance.

Implementation shall serve architecture.

Evidence shall support engineering.

Investigation shall establish institutional truth.

This hierarchy shall remain the permanent constitutional chain of authority throughout the lifetime of AXIOM.
