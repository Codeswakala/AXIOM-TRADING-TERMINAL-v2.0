## Operator decision

> **All active V1 governing documents remain binding on AXIOM V2. A V2 governing document may amend a V1 requirement only where it explicitly identifies the affected V1 document/section, states the replacement rule and effective scope, is approved through the established AXIOM governance process, and is recorded in the V2 amendment register. Historical V1 evidence and determinations remain immutable.**

---

## Operational effect

1. The active V1 constitutional hierarchy remains applicable to V2.
2. The V2 Product & Architecture Specification is a proposed V2 direction/design instrument. It does not, by itself, amend any V1 governing requirement or authorize implementation.
3. A future V2 amendment must identify, at minimum:
   - the affected V1 governing document and specific section(s);
   - the prior rule;
   - the replacement/amended rule;
   - the effective scope and date;
   - the approving authority and governing process;
   - the V2 amendment-register identifier.
4. No lower-tier planning document, Build Order, source code, test result, Delivery Report, or ITRGA evidence may silently supersede a V1 governing rule.
5. V1 historical Build Orders, Delivery Reports, ITRGA determinations, evidence, technical-debt history, and provenance remain immutable historical records.
6. This record does not authorize data providers, broker/exchange connectivity, paper trading, accounts, orders, positions, external AI, execution, production deployment, or production certification.
